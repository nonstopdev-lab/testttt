# Wavegram outreach templates (crypto projects)

Personalize the bracketed parts for every message. Do not send identical text in bulk.

## Email / LinkedIn (first touch)

Subject: Growing [Project]'s Telegram community

Hi [Name],

I saw [Project]'s Telegram community has [N members / is very active around X launch].
I'm [Your name] from Wavegram, a Telegram marketing tool that helps crypto projects
[one concrete benefit: e.g. schedule announcements across groups, run campaigns, track growth].

Would it be useful if I showed you how it would work for [Project]? Happy to share a
2-minute walkthrough, no call needed.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Wavegram link]

## Follow-up (after 4-5 days, once)

Hi [Name], quick follow-up on my note about Wavegram for [Project]'s Telegram.
If it's not relevant, no worries, just tell me and I won't message again.

## Rules
- Max 30-50 messages/day per account; Telegram limits cold DMs.
- Always include an easy opt-out line. Never message anyone twice after a "no".
- Prefer email or the project's official contact; DM only the community/marketing lead.
